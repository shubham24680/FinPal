FinPal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026 · Applies to FinPal v1.0.0

In short: FinPal keeps your data on your phone. We do not create accounts, sync to the cloud, connect to your bank, or sell your information.

1. Who we are

FinPal is a personal finance app that helps you track income and expenses, review spending, and understand your money at a glance.

This policy explains what FinPal stores on your device for version 1.0.0, and what we do not collect. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. What FinPal stores

Everything you enter stays on your device. FinPal does not send this information to our servers — FinPal v1.0.0 has no backend account system.

On your device, FinPal may store:

  • Profile details — display name, gender, date of birth, and the avatar you choose.
  • Transactions — income and expense entries you add, including amount, date, category, payment method, and optional notes.
  • Preferences — categories and payment methods you use or customize, plus a first-visit flag for onboarding.

FinPal does not ask for a password, email login, bank credentials, or permission to access your bank accounts.

3. How that information is used

Your data is used only to run the app on your device:

  • Show balances, history, and spending charts.
  • Personalize your profile and categories.
  • Warn you when spending goes over your recorded income.

We do not use your financial records for advertising, profiling across other apps, or selling lists.

4. Where your data lives

FinPal stores information locally on your phone using on-device storage. If you clear the app’s data or uninstall FinPal, that local information is removed from the device.

Because data stays on your phone, you control it. Keep your device locked and up to date — that is the main protection for your records.

5. Sharing and third parties

In FinPal v1.0.0, we do not share your profile or transaction data with third-party services. There is no cloud sync, analytics SDK, or ad network built into this version.

We do not sell your personal information. We would only disclose data if required by law — and even then, we typically have no remote copy of your FinPal records to hand over.

6. Your choices

You can:

  • Edit or delete transactions and profile details inside the app.
  • Clear app data from your phone’s system settings.
  • Uninstall FinPal to remove locally stored information.

7. Children’s privacy

FinPal is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help you understand how to remove local data.

8. Changes

If FinPal’s data practices change in a later version (for example, optional cloud or AI features), we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date. This page describes FinPal v1.0.0.
