Privacy Policy

FinPal
Last updated: 7 September 2026
Applies to FinPal version 1.0.0

FinPal is a personal finance app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores on your device, how it is used, and what we do not collect.

In short: your data stays on your phone. FinPal v1.0.0 does not create accounts, sync to the cloud, connect to your bank, or sell your information.

1. Who we are

FinPal is developed by Shubham Patel (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The app helps you track income and expenses, organise transactions, and view spending summaries on your own device.

By using FinPal, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. What FinPal stores

Everything you enter stays on your device. FinPal v1.0.0 has no backend account system and does not upload your records to our servers.

On your device, FinPal may store:

  • Profile details — display name, gender, date of birth, and any profile picture you choose.
  • Transactions — income and expense entries you add, including amount, date, category, payment method, optional notes, and any receipt photo you attach.
  • Preferences — categories and payment methods you create or customise, theme and currency settings, and whether you have completed onboarding.

FinPal does not ask for a password, email login, bank credentials, or permission to access your bank accounts.

3. Camera and photos

FinPal may request access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to set a profile picture or attach a receipt to a transaction. Access is requested at that moment. Nothing is read until you select or take an image.

When you pick an image, FinPal copies it into the app’s private storage on your device so the file is not lost if the system clears temporary cache. That copy is not uploaded. It stays on your phone and is removed when you delete the profile picture, delete the related transaction, clear the app’s data, or uninstall FinPal.

FinPal does not scan, index, or read other photos on your device. It does not keep ongoing access to your gallery beyond what you select.

On Android, this may involve permissions such as Camera, access to images, or (on older Android versions) storage access for selecting a photo. You can deny these permissions; features that need them will not work until you allow access.

4. How that information is used

Your data is used only to run FinPal on your device, including to:

  • Show balances, history, and spending charts.
  • Personalise your profile and categories.
  • Reflect spending relative to recorded income.

We do not use your financial records for advertising, profiling across other apps, or selling lists.

5. Where your data lives

FinPal stores information locally on your phone. If you clear the app’s data or uninstall FinPal, that local information is removed from the device.

Because data stays on your phone, you control it. Protect your device with a lock screen and keep the system up to date. There is no cloud backup in FinPal v1.0.0. If you lose the device or clear the data without your own backup, the records are gone.

6. Sharing and third parties

In FinPal v1.0.0, we do not share your profile or transaction data with third-party services. This version does not include cloud sync, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising networks that collect your FinPal records.

We do not sell your personal information. We would only disclose information if required by law. Even then, we typically have no remote copy of your FinPal records to hand over.

Opening Privacy Policy, Terms, or support links may launch your browser or email app. Those apps have their own privacy practices.

7. Your choices

You can:

  • Edit or delete transactions and profile details inside the app.
  • Use Clear Data in FinPal settings (where available) or clear app data from your phone’s system settings.
  • Uninstall FinPal to remove locally stored information.
  • Deny camera or photo permissions in system settings.

8. Children’s privacy

FinPal is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we can help you remove local data.

9. Changes

If FinPal’s data practices change in a later version (for example, optional cloud sync or other networked features), we will update this Privacy Policy and the “Last updated” date. This page describes FinPal v1.0.0.
