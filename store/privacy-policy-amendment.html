• Transactions — income and expense entries you add, including amount, date, category, payment method, optional notes, and any receipt photo you attach.

    • 3. Camera and photos

    FinPal asks for access to your camera or photo library only at the moment you choose to set a profile picture or attach a receipt to a transaction. The picker is opened by you, and nothing is read until you select an image.

    When you pick an image, FinPal copies it into the app's own private storage on your device so the picture does not disappear later. That copy is never uploaded — it stays on your phone and is deleted when you remove the profile picture, delete the transaction, or clear the app's data.

    FinPal does not scan, index, or read any other photos on your device, and it requests no ongoing access to your gallery.
